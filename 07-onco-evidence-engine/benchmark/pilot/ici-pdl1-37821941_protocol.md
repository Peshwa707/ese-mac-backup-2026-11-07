# Pilot #2 protocol draft — Blood-based PD-L1 in NSCLC on ICI therapy (PMID 37821941)

Source: Cui Q et al., World J Surg Oncol 2023;21(1):318. DOI 10.1186/s12957-023-03215-2. PROSPERO CRD42023402077. Protocol-only (no results) — for the engine compiler.

**Review question (PICO).** In NSCLC patients undergoing ICI therapy (mono or combination), is blood-based PD-L1 — soluble (sPD-L1), exosomal (exoPD-L1), and CTC PD-L1, measured pre/post-treatment — associated with overall survival (OS) and progression-free survival (PFS), expressed as hazard ratios (HR, 95% CI)?

**Databases & search.** PubMed, Embase, Cochrane Library; English-language; **search cutoff Feb 23, 2023**. Concept = NSCLC terms × ICI/PD-(L)1 terms × blood/liquid-biopsy + marker terms (sPD-L1, exoPD-L1, circulating/CTC PD-L1). Reference lists hand-searched.

**Eligibility.** Include: NSCLC on ICI mono/combination; reports correlation of a blood-based PD-L1 marker with OS/PFS as HR + 95% CI (HR from Kaplan–Meier if not reported directly; multivariate preferred). Exclude: case reports, reviews, meta-analyses, conference abstracts, letters; non-downloadable full text; animal; non-English.

**Designs.** Prospective and retrospective observational cohorts.

**Quality.** Newcastle–Ottawa Scale (≥7 high).

**Analysis.** Pooled HR via inverse-variance; heterogeneity Cochran Q + I² (I²<50%/P>0.10 = low); subgroups by time point (pre/post), sample size, region; publication bias Begg + Egger.

Data-quality note: search cutoff explicit (Feb 23 2023). Included-study PMIDs NOT enumerated in the available text — `expected_included_pmids` left empty.
