# DSM-5 Neurodevelopmental Criteria Evidence Package

**Status:** DRAFT — no clinical conclusions are unlocked until both independent AI reviews, named human Reviewer 2, reconciliation, and qualified clinical sign-off are recorded.

## Review sequence

1. Codex AI Reviewer 1 completes its evidence pass.
2. Claude secondary AI reviewer completes an independent cross-check.
3. A named human expert performs Reviewer 2 review and reconciliation.
4. A qualified clinician records the separate clinical sign-off.

AI identities must never be recorded in the human Reviewer 2 field.

## Plain-language purpose

This review asks how well each mapped DSM-5 criterion is measured, whether it helps distinguish overlapping diagnoses, how it relates to real-world functioning, and what mechanisms or supports may change symptoms or function.

## Outcome separation

Report symptom change, daily function, academic/work performance, participation, quality of life, harms, and durability in separate rows. Improvement in one row must not be presented as proof of improvement in another.

## Required limitations

- Diagnostic criteria are review units, not independently established truths.
- Mechanistic evidence does not establish treatment efficacy.
- A lack of evidence is not evidence against a criterion.
- AI output is a draft; human review and clinical authorization remain separate gates.
