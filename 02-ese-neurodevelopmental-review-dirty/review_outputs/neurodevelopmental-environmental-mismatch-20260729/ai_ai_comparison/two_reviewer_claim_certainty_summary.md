# Two-Reviewer Claim Certainty Summary

**Review:** Neurodevelopmental environmental mismatch rapid review
**Reviewers:** Primary Codex-driven review and blinded Claude secondary-AI review
**Scope:** 66 protocol claims, 980 screening occurrences, 98 extraction occurrences, 93 unique selected PubMed records
**Status:** Draft AI–AI synthesis; Human Reviewer 2, full-text review, formal risk-of-bias assessment, and clinical sign-off remain pending.

## Executive conclusion

No claim should be described as clinically “proven” from this artifact. The review is an abstract-level, rapid, AI-assisted evidence synthesis, and neither reviewer is a human reference standard.

The most defensible summary is:

| Category | Count | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Most supported / provisional | 7 | Both reviewers marked the claim `supported`; reported certainty was moderate for six and high for one. These are the strongest current signals, not proof. |
| Intermediate / partly supported | 46 | Evidence supports part of the claim, or reviewers differ between partial support, support, and indeterminacy. These require bounded wording and often human adjudication. |
| Indeterminate / insufficient evidence | 10 | Both reviewers judged the claim indeterminate, generally with very-low certainty. The evidence set does not answer the question. |
| Unsupported or not advanced | 3 | One claim was unsupported by both reviewers; two were indeterminate for the primary reviewer and unsupported for Claude. These should not be advanced as conclusions. |

The two reviewers agreed on the claim verdict for 78.8% of claims, but certainty agreement was only 62.1% and directness-to-case agreement was only 3.0%. Agreement therefore supports reproducible disagreement routing, not clinical validity.

## How to read the categories

- **Supported** means the reviewer judged that the available evidence supports the claim as worded, subject to its stated limitations.
- **Partially supported** means some component is supported, but scope, directness, magnitude, causality, population, or competing explanations limit the claim.
- **Indeterminate** means the eligible evidence does not answer the claim sufficiently.
- **Unsupported** means the available evidence does not support the claim as stated.
- **Certainty** is the reviewer-reported label (`high`, `moderate`, `low`, or `very low`), not a completed GRADE assessment.
- **Directness** refers to applicability to the case/question, not merely whether a study exists.

### Appropriate wording for this tier

Use wording such as **“supported at group level,” “associated with,” “consistent with,”** or **“provisional evidence supports.”** Do not use “proven,” “established in this individual,” “caused by,” or “therefore requires treatment.”

Common limitations across this tier were:

- group-level associations being mistaken for individual explanations;
- cross-sectional or self-report evidence;
- small or uncontrolled samples;
- lack of adult, longitudinal, or diagnosis-specific data;
- competing explanations not measured together;
- environmental effects being plausible but not causally isolated; and
- interventions improving rated symptoms or function without durable objective real-world evidence.

## 5. Cross-cutting synthesis

The two reviews converge on a cautious, plural, state-sensitive interpretation:

1. ADHD-related symptoms and impairment can persist across development and affect multiple domains.
2. Sleep, mood, medication, substances, environment, and comorbidity can alter observed performance and must be measured as competing or interacting explanations.
3. External structure and environmental modification can change observable functioning, but this does not prove a stable hidden impairment or a single compensation mechanism.
4. High achievement, intelligence, creativity, masking, or hyperfocus cannot be used alone to confirm or exclude a neurodevelopmental diagnosis.
5. Group-level evidence can prioritize hypotheses and safety-relevant modifiers but cannot determine the cause of an individual’s presentation.
6. The strongest unifying conclusion is a boundary statement: **the evidence supports heterogeneous, context-sensitive associations more strongly than it supports one coherent individual-level explanatory model.**

## 6. What remains unresolved

The summary must not be exported as a final clinical conclusion because:

- the comparison is abstract-level and rapid;
- full-text screening and extraction remain pending;
- formal risk-of-bias and certainty-of-evidence assessment remain pending;
- directness-to-case agreement was extremely low;
- neither AI reviewer is a reference standard;
- shared corpus and shared protocol can produce correlated errors; and
- Human Reviewer 2 and clinical sign-off remain pending.

## 7. Recommended human-adjudication order

1. Resolve the nine material claim disagreements: `H1.2`, `H2.4`, `H5.4`, `H4.2`, `H7.N1`, `H8.1`, `H8.4`, `G3`, and `G5`.
2. Review all claims with low directness-to-case agreement, regardless of verdict agreement.
3. Verify full text and study design for the seven most-supported claims before using “moderate” or “high” certainty language.
4. Confirm whether partially supported claims are appropriate for the intended question or only for contextual background.
5. Preserve indeterminate claims as explicit evidence gaps.
6. Require a qualified clinical signatory before any patient-facing or clinical-decision-support wording.

## 8. Engine representation requirements

The SOA Evidence Engine should preserve, for every claim:

- the exact claim wording and version;
- both reviewer verdicts and certainty labels;
- directness-to-case judgments;
- calibration confidence;
- cited source IDs and evidence roles;
- whether evidence was abstract-only or full text;
- material-disagreement status;
- human adjudication decision and rationale;
- clinical sign-off state; and
- export eligibility.

The engine should use separate states for `supported`, `partially_supported`, `indeterminate`, `unsupported`, and `human_adjudicated`. It should not collapse these into a binary true/false field or label AI agreement as proof.
