## 1. Background & objective
This module evaluates motivational modulation, person-environment fit, hyperfocus, creativity, divergent thinking, and proposed neurodevelopmental strengths while actively testing nonspecific and selection-based explanations.

## 2. Review question (PICO)
| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Population** | Adolescents and adults with ADHD, autism-spectrum disorder or traits, and relevant control groups. |
| **Exposure/Intervention** | Interest, novelty, reward, feedback, urgency, autonomy, person-environment fit, workplace or educational fit, hyperfocus, creativity, divergent thinking, or pattern recognition. |
| **Comparator** | Low-interest or routine tasks, low-reward conditions, standardized environments, controls, or alternative explanatory variables. |
| **Designs** | systematic review, meta-analysis, experimental, cohort, cross-sectional, qualitative |

## 3. Eligibility criteria
**Include (all must hold):**
- Adolescent or adult ADHD/autism sample or validated trait analysis.
- Empirically examines motivation, task context, environmental fit, hyperfocus, creativity, divergent thinking, or pattern recognition.
- Reports a comparative, within-person, or systematically synthesized result.

**Exclude (any excludes):**
- Advocacy-only or conceptual claim without empirical outcome data.
- No separable ADHD/autism group or validated trait analysis.
- Purely pediatric educational study without developmental relevance.
- No outcome relevant to claims H3, H6, or H10.
