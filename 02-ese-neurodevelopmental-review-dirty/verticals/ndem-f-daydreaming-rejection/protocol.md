## 1. Background & objective
This module evaluates associations and temporal evidence linking maladaptive or immersive daydreaming with loneliness, rejection, bullying, shame, boredom, trauma, anxiety, and neurodevelopmental traits, while testing normative fantasy and dissociative or compulsive alternatives.

## 3. Eligibility criteria
**Include (all must hold):**
- Adolescent or adult empirical study of maladaptive or immersive daydreaming, or rejection/anxiety mediation relevant to neurodevelopmental functioning.
- Uses an operational definition or validated/explicit measure.
- Reports association, temporal, mediation, qualitative-function, or impairment outcomes.

**Exclude (any excludes):**
- Normative mind-wandering study with no maladaptive/immersive daydreaming or emotional-burden relevance.
- Opinion, popular media, commercial page, or unsourced narrative.
- No operational definition of the focal construct.
- No outcome relevant to claims H9 or H12.
